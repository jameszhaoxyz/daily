=== 2026-08-14 | A note on notation

Good notation is compression. When I write $f: X \to Y$ I have said, in six
characters, what a paragraph would say badly.

The classic example is the Gaussian integral:

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}$$

Nothing in that line is decoration. Every symbol is load-bearing, and the
result is memorable precisely because it is short.

=== 2026-08-13 | On writing every day

The hardest part is not the writing. It is deciding that today's thought is
allowed to be small.

A daily note is not an essay. It is a receipt for having paid attention.

=== 2026-08-12 | The value of small tools

A tool that does one thing keeps its shape in your head. You can predict it,
compose it, and throw it away.

Large tools ask you to learn them. Small tools ask you to use them.

=== 2026-08-11 | Notes from a slow morning

Slowness is a measurement device. When nothing is urgent you find out what you
actually wanted to do.
